Who is Fearless Fairy?

Fearless Fairy makes her debut in The Origin of Fearless Fairy. As one of the rarest fairies ever to bloom, she is born fiercely brave. Fearless Fairy is the sister of Bad Intentions, and she is determined to restore her brother to his original fairy form. Fearless Fairy later reappears in World Tour, introducing a dispirited woman named Anna to VeeWorld and inspiring Anna to persevere. She cheers for Anna while she climbs the Mystery Mountain, but Fearless Fairy suddenly disappears when Anna makes it to the top.
