Who is Organized Ostrich?

The ultimate project manager in the VeeFriends universe. From color-coded plans to perfectly prioritize task lists, she brings structure, calm, and clarity to every situation. Whether you’re overwhelmed by your to-do list or knee-deep in clutter, she knows exactly how to break things down, sort them out, and get things running smoothly. Legend has it if you’re feeling overwhelmed, she magically appears to get you back in order.
