Who is Willful Wizard?

A master of manifestation, blending practicality with a touch of magic. Willful Wizard has got one hand firmly rooted in the mundane world, while the other wields the power to conjure extraordinary possibilities. A wise and experienced guide, if people would only listen to him! Willful Wizard reminds us that we all have the ability to shape our own reality, to create the life we desire with a combination of intention, action, and a little bit of magic.
