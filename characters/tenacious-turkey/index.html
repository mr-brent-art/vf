Who is Tenacious Turkey?

He is a model of perseverance and determination. Once Tenacious Turkey sets his sights on a goal, he's like a dialed in dog with a bone, refusing to let go until he achieves it. He's got the stamina to keep going, even when the going gets tough, and he inspires others to do the same. He's a reminder that quitting is the only way to lose and that with enough grit and determination, anything is possible.
